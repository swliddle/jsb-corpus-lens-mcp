TEXTUAL CHANGES — D&C Section 20, Evening and Morning Star (1832)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

▶ Changes from Revelation Book 1 (ca-1830-04) → Evening and Morning Star (1832):

  1. PHRASE ADDITION (verse 4):
     EARLIER: "and this according to the grace of our Lord & saveiour Jesus Christ"
     LATER:   "and this according to the grace of God the Father, and, our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ"
     → This addition explicitly includes "God the Father" as a source of grace, altering the theological statement.

  2. WORD CHANGE (verse 5):
     EARLIER: "God ministered unto him by an Holy Angel"
     LATER:   "God visited him by an holy angel"
     → The change from "ministered" (implying aid or service) to "visited" (implying a direct encounter) alters the description of the divine interaction.

  3. PHRASE DELETION (verse 9):
     EARLIER: "God doth Minister inspire men & call them to his Holy work"
     LATER:   "God doth inspire men and call them to his holy work"
     → The removal of the word "Minister" clarifies the sentence by eliminating a redundant verb, focusing God's action on inspiring and calling.

  4. PHRASE ADDITION (verse 17):
     EARLIER: "[no similar text]"
     LATER:   "and that he is all power, and all wisdom, and all understanding,"
     → This new phrase adds a significant doctrinal description of God's attributes that was not present in the earlier version.

  5. WORD CHANGE (verse 18):
     EARLIER: "gave unto the Children of men a commandment"
     LATER:   "gave unto the children of men commandments"
     → Changing the singular "commandment" to the plural "commandments" broadens the scope from a single principle to a set of laws given by God.

  6. WORD CHANGE (verse 24):
     EARLIER: "were Baptized in his Holy name"
     LATER:   "were baptized into his holy name"
     → The change from "in" to "into" adds a sense of entering a new state or covenant relationship, a more dynamic action than the static "in".

  7. WORD CHANGE (verse 25):
     EARLIER: "which truly testify of him in all things"
     LATER:   "which truly testified of him in all things"
     → The change from present tense "testify" to past tense "testified" alters the timeframe of the holy prophets' witness concerning Christ.

  8. WORD CHANGE (verse 33):
     EARLIER: "let the Church take heed & pray always lest ye fall into temptation"
     LATER:   "let the Church take heed and pray always, lest they enter into temptations"
     → The text changes from a direct address ("ye") to a third-person reference ("they") and from an outcome ("fall") to an action ("enter").

  9. PHRASE ADDITION (verse 35):
     EARLIER: "agreeable to the Revelations of John"
     LATER:   "agreeable to the Revelations of Jesus Christ which was signified by his angel unto John"
     → This addition clarifies that the ultimate source of the revelation is Jesus Christ, with an angel and John acting as intermediaries.

  10. PHRASE DELETION (verse 35):
     EARLIER: "by the ministering of the Holy Ghost Angels"
     LATER:   "by the ministering of angels"
     → The removal of "the Holy Ghost" separates the ministering of angels from the ministering of the Holy Ghost, which were conflated in the earlier text.

  11. PHRASE DELETION (verse 46):
     EARLIER: "ordain other Priests Teachers & Deacons"
     LATER:   "ordain priests, teachers, and deacons"
     → Removing the word "other" changes the duty from ordaining additional officers to the general duty of ordaining officers.

  12. WORD CHANGE (verse 56):
     EARLIER: "evry member does his duty"
     LATER:   "all the members their duty"
     → This change shifts the focus from the responsibility of each individual member ("evry member his duty") to a collective church responsibility ("all the members their duty").

  13. WORD CHANGE (verse 68):
     EARLIER: "so that all things may be done in order"
     LATER:   "so that all things shall be done in order"
     → The change from "may" (which implies permission or possibility) to "shall" (which implies a command or requirement) makes the instruction more forceful.

  14. PHRASE DELETION (verse 73):
     EARLIER: "I baptize thee in the name of Jesus Christ the Father & of the Son & of the Holy Ghost amen"
     LATER:   "I baptize you in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen."
     → The name "Jesus Christ" is removed from the baptismal prayer, a significant alteration to the ordinance's wording.

  15. PHRASE ADDITION (verse 74):
     EARLIER: "[no similar text]"
     LATER:   "And the manner of administering the sacrament are to be done as is written in the Book of Morman."
     → This new sentence is added, directing the church to the Book of Mormon for the procedure of the sacrament, an instruction not present before.

  16. WORD CHANGE (verse 79):
     EARLIER: "that they do it in Rememberance of the blood of thy Son"
     LATER:   "that they may do it in Rememberance of the blood of thy Son"
     → Changing "do" to "may do" alters the phrasing from a statement of fact about the action to a statement of purpose or intent for the action.

  17. WORD CHANGE (verse 84):
     EARLIER: "send one or more of their Teachers to attend the several conferences"
     LATER:   "send one of their priests to attend the several conferences"
     → This change alters which priesthood office is required to represent a congregation at conference, from "Teachers" to "priests," and removes the "or more" option.

  18. WORD CHANGE (verse 84):
     EARLIER: "or send by the hand of some Priest"
     LATER:   "or send by the hand of some elder"
     → The priesthood office authorized to carry the list of new members to the conference is changed from a Priest to an Elder.

  19. PHRASE DELETION (verse 86):
     EARLIER: "a regular member & in good standing"
     LATER:   "a member and in good standing"
     → The removal of the word "regular" simplifies the description of a member's status on a letter of recommendation.
