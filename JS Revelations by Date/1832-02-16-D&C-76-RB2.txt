[heading] Vision, 16 February 1832 D&C 76 — — — The Vision — — — — — A vision of Joseph & Sidney Rigdon February 16th. 1832 given in Portage County Hiram Township State of Ohio in North America which they saw concerning the church of the first born and concerning the economy of God and his vast creation througout all eternity
