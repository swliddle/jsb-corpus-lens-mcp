[heading] Section 31 • Revelation, February 1829 D&C 4 SECTION XXXI. Revelation to Joseph Smith, Sen. given February, 1829.

1
Now, behold, a marvellous work is about to come forth among the children of men,
2
therefore, O ye that embark in the service of God, see that ye serve him with all your heart, might, mind and strength, that ye may stand blameless before God at the last day:
3
therefore, if ye have desires to serve God, ye are called to the work,
4
for behold the field is white already to harvest, and lo, he that thrusteth in his sickle with his might, the same layeth up in store that he perish not, but bringeth salvation to his soul,
5
and faith, hope, charity, and love, with an eye single to the glory of God, qualifies him for the work.
6
2 Remember faith, virtue, knowledge, temperance, patience, brotherly kindness, godliness, charity, humility, diligence.
7
Ask and ye shall receive, knock and it shall be opened unto you: Amen.
