[heading] Revelation, 29 October 1831 D&C 66 A Revelation to Wm. E Mc lellin Recd. Oct. 29th. 1831

1
Behold thus saith the Lord unto you my Servent William E. Mclelin blessed are you in as much as you have turned away from your iniquities & have received my truths saith the Lord your redeemer the saveiour of the World of as many as believe on my name
2
verily I say unto you blessed are you for receiving mine everlasting truths Covenant even the fullness of my gospel sent forth unto the Children of men that they might have life & be made partakers of the glories which was to be revealed in the last days as it was written by the Prophets & Apostles in days of old
3
verily I say unto you my Servant Wm. that you are Clean but not all repent therefore of those things which are not pleasing in my sight saith the Lord for the Lord will shew them unto you
4
what I & now verily I the Lord will shew unto you what I will concerning you or what is my will concerning you
5
Behold verily I say unto you that it is my will that you should proclaim my Gospel from land to land & from City to City yea in those regions page torn round about where it hath not been proclaimed
6
tarry not many days in this place go not up unto the land of Zion but in as much as you can send send otherwise think not of thy property
7
go unto the Eastern lands bear testimony unto every people & in every Place & in their synnagogues reasoning with the people
8
let my Servant Samuel Smith go with thee you & forsake him not & give him thine instructions & he that is faithfull shall be made strong in every place & I the Lord will go with you
9
lay your hands upon the sick & they shall recover return not untill I the Lord shall send you be patiant in afflictions ask & ye shall receive knock & it shall be opened unto you
10
seek not to be Cumbered forsake all unrighteousness commit not Adultery a temptation with which thou hast been troubled
11
keep these Sayings true & faithfull & thou shalt magnify thine office & push many people to Zion with songs of everlasting Joy upon their heads
12
continue in those things unto the end & you shall have a crown of eternal life on the right hand of my father who is full of grace & truth
13
verily thus saith the Lord your God your redeemer even Jesus Christ Amen
