that he would not lie with her, lest he
should raise up seed unto his brother.
16And he turned unto her by the
way and said, “Go to I pray thee, let
me come in and lie with thee” (for he
knew not that she was his daughter-in-

give me that thou mayest come in and
lie with me?”
18And he said, “What pledge shall I
give thee?” And she said, “Thy signet
and thy bracelets and thy staff that is
in thine hand.” And he gave it her and
came in and slept with her, and she
conceived by him.
