Ghost, returned from Jordan and was

2And after forty days the devil came
unto him to tempt him.

ing, and when they were ended he af-

unto him, “If thou be the Son of God,
command this stone that it be made
bread.” 4And Jesus answered him, say-
ing, “It is written that man shall not
live by bread alone but by every word
of God.”a

words were with power. 33And in the

this! For with authority and power he
commandeth the unclean spirits and
they come out.” 37And the fame of him
went out in every place round about.

of the synagogue and entered into

mother was taken with a great fever,
and they besought him for to heal her.
39And he stood over her and rebuked

ately she arose and administered unto
them.

that he was Christ.
