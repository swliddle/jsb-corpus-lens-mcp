[heading] Revelation, 6 December 1832 D&C 86 Revelation given December 6, 1832 Kirtland Ohio explaining the parable of the Wheat & Tears tares

1
Verily thus saith the Lord unto you my servants concerning the parable of the Wheat & of the Tears.
2
Behold verily I say that the field was the world, & the apostles were the sowers of} the seed;
3
& after they have fallen asleep, the great persecutor of the church, the apostate, the whore, even Babylon that maketh all nations to drink of her cup, in whose heart the enemy even Satan sitteth to reign. Behold he soweth the Tears; wherefore the tears choke the wheat & drive the church into the wilderness.
4
But behold in the last days: even now while the Lord is begining to bring forth the word, & the blade is springing up & is yet tender:
5
Behold verily I say to unto you, the angels are crying unto the Lord day & night, who are ready & waiting to be sent forth to reap down the field.
6
But the Lord saith unto them, pluck not up the Tears while the blade is yet tender (for verily your faith is weak) lest you destroy the wheat also,
7
therefore let the wheat & the tears grow together untill the Harvest is fully ripe Then ye shall first gather out the wheat from the Tears, & after the gathering of the wheat, behold & lo the tears are bound in bundles, & the field remaineth to be burned.
8
Therefore thus saith the Lord unto you with whom the priesthood hath continued through the lineage of your fathers:
9
For ye are lawful heirs according to the flesh & have been hid from the world, with Christ in God.
10
Therefore your life & the priesthood hath remained & must needs remain through you & your lineage untill the resteration of all things, spoken by the mouth of all the holy prophets since the world began:
11
Therefore, blessed are ye if ye continue in my goodness, a light unto the Gentiles, & through this priesthood a saveior unto the world my people Israel, the Lord hath said it.
