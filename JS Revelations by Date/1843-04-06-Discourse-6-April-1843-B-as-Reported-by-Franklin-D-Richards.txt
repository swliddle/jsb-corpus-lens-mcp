═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

Joseph said
When he was asking to know of the time of Christs second Coming he obtained for answer my son if thou shalt live untill thou art Eighty five years old thou shalt see the son of man on Earth [p. [16]]

Joseph was born in Windsor County Vermont Dec 23 1805 He is therefore now 37 years old last Dec which leaves 48 years yet to transpire untill the time of Promise that Joseph should see Christ [p. [17]]
