Verse range: Joseph Smith—History 1:54.

DATE NOTE: this verse covers the INTERVAL between the first hill visit (22 September 1823) and
the receipt of the plates (22 September 1827) — the annual interviews of 1824, 1825, and 1826.
It is filed at 1824-09-22, the first annual return, so it sorts between the two events it spans.

═══ HISTORICAL INTRODUCTION ═══

Having been forbidden to take the plates in 1823 and told to return to the same place
annually, Joseph did so at the end of each year until the appointed time. This single verse is
the whole record of that four-year interval — three annual interviews with the same messenger,
in 1824, 1825, and 1826.

What it reports of those interviews is instruction rather than event: at each meeting Joseph
received "instruction and intelligence" concerning what the Lord was going to do, and how and in
what manner his kingdom was to be conducted in the last days. The content of that instruction is
not given here. The verse establishes the pattern — annual, at a fixed place, with the same
messenger, over four years — and the subject matter, without recording what was said.

═══ ACCOUNT TEXT ═══

54 Accordingly, as I had been commanded, I went at the end of each year, and at each time I found the same messenger there, and received instruction and intelligence from him at each of our interviews, respecting what the Lord was going to do, and how and in what manner his kingdom was to be conducted in the last days.
