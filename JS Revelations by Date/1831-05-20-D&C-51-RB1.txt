═══ D&C 51 — Revelation Book 1 ═══

1 Revelation, 20 May 1831 [D&C 51] 54 Commandment —— 54 <Com> A Revelation given to the Bishop at Thompson Ohio May 20th. 1831 concerning the property of the Church &c
2 Hearken unto me saith the lord your God & I will speak unto my Servent Edward [Partridge] & give unto him directions for it must needs be that he receive directions how to organize this people
3 for it must needs be that they are organized according to my laws if otherwise they will be cut off
4 wherefore let my Servent Edward receive the properties of this People which have covenanted with me to obey the Laws which I have given & let my Servent Edward receive the money as it shall be laid before him according to the covenant & go & obtain a deed or Article of this land unto himself for I have appointed him to receive these things & thus through him the Properties of this Church shall be covenanted unto me
5 wherefore let my Servent Edward & those whom he has chosen in whom I am well pleased appoint unto this People their portion every man alike according to their families according to their wants & their needs
6 & let my servent Edward when he shall appoint a man his portion give unto him a writing that shall secure unto him his portion that he shall hold it of the Church untill he transgress & is not counted worthy by the Church voice of the Church according to the laws to belong to the Church
7 [not present in this edition]
8 & thus all things shall be made sure according to the laws of the land
9 & let that which belongeth to this people be appointed unto this people
10 & the money which is left unto this people let there be an agent appointed unto this people to take the money to provide food & raiment according to the wants of this people
11 & let every man deal honestly & be alike among you this People & receive alike that ye may be one even as I have commanded you
12 & let that which belongeth to this people not be taken & given unto that of another church
13 wherefore if another Church would receive money of this Church let them pay unto this church again according as they shall agree
14 & this shall be done through the Bishop or the agent which shall be appointed by the voice of the church
15 & again let the Bishop appoint a storehouse unto this Church & let all things both in in money & in meat which is more then is needful for the want of this People be kept in the hands of the Bishop
16 & let him also reserve unto himself for his own wants & for the wants of his family as he shall be employed in doing this Business
17 & thus I grant unto this People a privelige of organizeing themselves according to my laws
18 & I consecrate unto them this land for a little season untill I the Lord shall provide for them otherwise & command them to go hence
19 & the hour & the day is not given unto them wherefore let them act upon this land as for years & this shall turn unto them for their good
20 Behold this shall be an example unto my Servent Edward in other places in all Churches
21 & whoso is found a faithful & Just & a wise stewart shall enter into the Joy of his lord & shall inherit eternal life
22 verily I say unto you I am Jesus Christ who cometh quickly in an hour you think not even so Amen
