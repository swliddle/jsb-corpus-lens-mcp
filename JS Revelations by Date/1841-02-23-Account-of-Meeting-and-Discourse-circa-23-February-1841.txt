Next Metting first on the Authantisitty of the book of Mormon 2 on Education— 3d Davis on Charity— 4 on Manners—
Joseph said he Never wanted to [p. [12]]

hear a man snore Louder than he could shout in battle— he Did Not want a man say O Joseph how I Love you &cc & when the time of Danger come forsake him [p. [13]]
