2. P.M. At the .
John Finch— spoke on the 3d principle mentioned yesterday 10 past. 3.— Joseph spoke 5 minutes. told an anecdote of . and got up a community. at . big fish eat up the little. did not beleive the doctrine.— &c. Finch replied— a few minuts.— said he.— “I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness. & I am the Spiritual prophet. Mr Smith the Temporal Prophet.” [p. [94]]
