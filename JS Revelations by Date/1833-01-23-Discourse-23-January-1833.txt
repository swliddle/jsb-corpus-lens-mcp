═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

The President said after he had of the , as I have done so do ye wash ye therefee [therefore] one anothers feet pronouncing at the same time through the power of the Holy Ghost that the Elders were all clean from the blood of this generation but that those among them who should sin wilfully after they were thus cleansed and up unto eternal life should be given over unto the buffettings of Satan until the day of redemption [p. 8]
