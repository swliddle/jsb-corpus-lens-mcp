1 July 1832 (February 1835) Issue Revelation, 7 August 1831 [D&C 59] REVELATIONS. 4COMMANDMENT FOR KEEPING THE SABBATH, -[Given August 7, 1831.]-
2 Behold, saith the Lord, <blessed > are they who have come up unto this land with an eye single to my glory, according to my commandments;
3 <f>or the<y> that live<, and walk in them, faithfully,> shall inherit the earth <when it is prepared for them;> and <when they die they> shall rest from all their labors and their works shall follow them<:> and they shall receive a crown in the mansions of my Fatherwhich I have prepared<.>
4 Yea, blessed are they whose feet stand upon the land of Zion, who have obeyed my gospel<;> for they shall receive for their reward the good things of the earth<,> <a>nd it shall bring forth in its strength< for them>:
5 <a>nd they shall <also> be crowned with blessings from above, yea, and with commandments not a few<,> and with revelations in their time<—> they who are faithful and diligent before me<.>
6 Wherefore I give unto them a commandment, saying, thus: Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, with all thy might mind and strength<,> and in the name of Jesus Christ thou shalt serve him.<—>
7 Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself. Thou shall not steal<, n>either commit adultery, nor kill, nor do any thing like unto it.
8 Thou shalt thank the Lord thy God in all things.
9 Thou shalt offer a sacrifice unto the Lord thy God in righteousness; <e>ven that of a broken heart and a contrite spirit.
10 And that thou mayest more fully keep thyself unspotted from the world, thou shalt go to the house of prayer and offer up thy sacraments upon my holy day<;>
11 <f>or verily<,> this is a day appointed unto thee to rest from thy labors, and to pay thy devotions unto the Most High<.>
12 Nevertheless<,> thy vows sh<ould> be offered up in righteousness on all days, and at all times;
13 <b>ut remember<,> that on this, the Lord’s day, thou shalt offer thine oblations and thy sacraments unto the Most High, confessing thy sins unto thy brethren and before the Lord;
14 <a>nd on this day thou shalt do none other work, only let thy food be prepared with singleness of heart, that thy fasting may be perfect, or in other words, that thy joy may be full<;>
15 <for v>erily<,> this is fasting and prayer; or, in other words, rejoicing and prayer.
16 And inasmuch as ye do these things with thanksgiving, with cheerful hearts and countenances<—>not with much laughter for this is sin—but with a glad heart and a cheerful countenance<:>
17 <v>erily I say unto you, that inasmuch as ye do this, the fulness of the earth is yours; <t>he beasts of the field, and the fowls of the air, and that which climbeth upon the trees, and walketh upon the earth<,>
18 <y>ea, and the herb, and the good things which come <forth> of the earth, whether for food or raiment, or houses, or barns, or orchards, or gardens, or vineyards;
19 <y>ea, all things which come <forth >of the earth, in the season thereof, <are> made for the benefit and the use of man, both to please the eye and to gladden the heart<;>
20 <y>ea, for food and raiment, for taste and smell, to strengthen the body, and to enliven the soul<;>
21 <a>nd it pleases God that he has given all these things unto man<;> <f>or unto this end were they made, to be used with judgment, not to excess, neither by extortion:
22 <a>nd in nothing do<es> man offend God, or against none is his wrath kindled save those who confess not his hand in all things, and obey not his commandments.
23 Behold this is according to the law and the prophets<.> Wherefore<,> trouble me no more concerning this matter,
24 but learn that he who does the works of righteousness shall receive his reward, even peace in this world, and eternal life in the world to come.
25 I the Lord have spoken it, and the Spirit beareth record. Amen.
