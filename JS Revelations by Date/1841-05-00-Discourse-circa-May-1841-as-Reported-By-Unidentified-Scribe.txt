═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

By Joseph
Everlasting covenent was made between 3 personages before the organization of this earth; & relates to their dispensation of things to men on the earth. Theses personages according to Abrahams record are called God the first, the creator God the second, the redeemer, & God the third, the witness or Testator.— [p. [2]]
