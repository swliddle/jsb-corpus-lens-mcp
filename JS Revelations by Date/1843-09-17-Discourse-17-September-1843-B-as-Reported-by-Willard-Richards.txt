═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

P.M. Mr Blodget the unitarian minister preached was gratified with his [p. [98]]

sermon in general— but differd in opinion on some points— on which I freely ixpressed myself to his great satisfaction on perscutin [persecution] making the work spread.— by rooting up a flower garden or kicking back the sun. [10 lines blank] [p. [99]]
