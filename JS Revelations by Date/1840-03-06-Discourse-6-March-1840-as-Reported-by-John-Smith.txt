═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

March 6th 1840
Met to in Council Joseph & present who informed us that thus Saith the Lord you need not observe the Law of Consecration untill our case was Decided in Congress also to be very mild in our Difficulties with each other not have any trials in council if it could be avoided would to Lord that we could be at peace among ourselves [p. [60]]
