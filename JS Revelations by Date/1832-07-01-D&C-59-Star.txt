7 <T>hou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself<. T>hou shalt not steal<. N>either commit adultery<,> nor kill<,> nor do any thing like unto it<.>
8 <T>hou shalt thank the Lord thy God in all things<.>

10 <and> that thou mayest more fully keep thyself unspoted from the world<,> thou shalt go to the house of prayer and offer up thy <s>acraments upon my holy day<,>

17 verily I say<,> that inasmuch as ye do this the fulness of the <e>arth is yours<;> the beasts of the fields<, and> the fowls of the air<, and> that which climbeth upon the trees<, and> walketh upon the <e>arth<,>
