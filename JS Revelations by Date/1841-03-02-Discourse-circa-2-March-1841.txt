═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

Joseph said there was a diferance Between the vengeance that Belongeth to the Lord, & a man Defending himself or friend— of the Mamon of unrighteousness Joseph said the majority of man king [mankind] was ist & true a must Lavish his Goods on all around him & out of them he will find freinds in the hour of Distress for those that have been made Ritch By the Benevalance of their Rich friend of them there will be some that will do you Good in affliction— [p. [13]]
