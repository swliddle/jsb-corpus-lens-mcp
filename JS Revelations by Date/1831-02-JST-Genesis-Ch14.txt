Abram after that Lot was separated
from him, “Lift up now thine eyes and
look from the place where thou art,
northward and southward and east-

the covenant which I make with thee,
for it shall be an everlasting covenant.

which thou seest, to thee will I give it
and to thy seed forever. 16And I will
make thy seed as the dust of the earth
so that if a man can number the dust of
the earth, thy seed shall also be num-
bered. 17Arise, walk through the land
in the length of it and in the breadth
of it, for I will give it unto thee.” 18Then

dwelled in the plain of Mamre, which
was in Hebron, and built there an altar
unto the Lord.
