Next Meeting— Joseph said the Lord said that we should build our to his Name that we might be [p. [8]]

for the Dead— But if we Did it Not we should be Rejected & our Dead with us & this should Not be excepted [accepted]
Lectured on the 11th of Rom of the powers that be ordained of God the Law of Order in the heavenly Bodys as also in all animal Creation [p. [9]]
