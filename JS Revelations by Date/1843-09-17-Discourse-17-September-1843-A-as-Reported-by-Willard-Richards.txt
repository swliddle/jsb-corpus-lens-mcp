═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

Sunday 17th—— Septmbr was at meeting. gave some directions. & while preachd I took my part as Mayor outside of the assembly. to keep order. & set pattern for the under officers.— after prechig [preaching] gave some instuctin instruction about order in the congregation— men among women & women among men. horess [horses] in the assembly men & boys on the &c [p. [98]]
