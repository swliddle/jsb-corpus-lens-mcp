═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

Joseph again spoke and shewed that his Power was in the Doctrines he taught, and defied all men to upset it— he called aloud “as the voice of one bawling in the Wilderness Repent ye Repent ye for the Kingdom of God is at hand”— [p. [4]]
