[heading] Revelation, 4 December 1831–A D&C 72:1–8 Kirtland December 4th 1831

1
Harken and listen to the voice of the Lord Oh ye who have asembled yourselves together who are the high Priest of my church to whom the Kingdom and power is given
2
for verily Saith the Lord it is expedient in me for a bishop to be appointed unto you or of you unto the church in this part of the Lords viniard
3
and verily in this thing yea have done wisely for it is required of the Lord at the hand of every Steward to render an acount of his Stewardship both in time and in eternity
4
for he who is faithful and wise in time is acounted worthy to inherit the mantions prepareed of for him of my Father
5
Verily I Say unto you the Elders of the Church in this part of my vineyard Shall render an acount of his Stewardship unto the Bishop which Shall be appointed of me in this part of my Vinyard
6
these things Shall be had on record to be handed over to the Bishop in Zion
7
and the duty of the Bishop Shall be made known by the commandments which have been given and by the voice of the conference
8
and verily I Say unto you my Servent Knewel Newel K. Whitney is the man which Shall be appointed and ordained unto this power this is the will of the Lord your god your redeemer even So Amen——
9
The word of the Lord in addition to the Law which has been given making known the duty of the Bishop who has been ordained unto the church in this part of the vinyard which is verily this
10
to keep the Lords Store house to receive the funds of the church in this part of the vinyard
11
to take an account of the Elders as before has been commanded and to administer to their wants who Shall pay for what they receive inasmuch as they have wherewith to pay
12
that this also may be consecrated to the good of the good of the church to the poor and needy
13
and he who not wherewith to pay an acount Shall be taken and handed over to Bishop in Zion who Shall pay the debt out of that which the Lord Shall put into his hands
14
and they of the faithful who labour in Spiritual things in admineresting administering the gospel and the things of the Kingdom unto the churrch and unto the world Shall answer the debt unto the Bishop in Zin Zion
15
thus it cometh out of the church for acording to the Law every man that cometh up to Zion must lay all things before the Bishop in Zion
16
and now verily I Say unto you that every Elder in this part of the vinyard must give an acount of his Stewardship unto the Bishop in this part of the vinyrd
17
a certifycate frome the judge or Bishop in this part of the vinyard unto the Bishop in Zion rendereth every man excepted accepted and answereth all things for an inheritance and to be received as a wise Steward and as a faithful labourer
18
otherwise he shall not be accepted of the Bishop in Zion
19
And new now verily I Say unto you let every elder who Shall give an acount unto the Bishop of the church in this part of vinyard be recommended by the churches or churches in which he labours that he my may render himself and his acounts aproved in all things—
20
And again let my Servents who are appointed as Stewards of over the literary concerns of my Church have a clame for asistance upon the Bishop or Bishops in all things
21
that the revilations may be published and go forth unto the ends of the earth that they also may obtain funds which Shall benifit the church in all things
22
that they also may render themselves approved in all things and be acounted as wise Stewards——
23
And now behold this Shall be an ensampile for all the extensive branches of my church in whatsoever language land they Shall be established and now I make an end of my Sayings at this time Amen——
24
A few words in addition to the Laws of the kingdom respecting the members of the church they who are appointed by the holy spirit to go up unto Zion and they who are appointed priveleged to go up unto Zion
25
let them carry up unto the Bishop a certificate from three Elders of the church, or a certificate from the Bishop
26
otherwise he who shall go up unto the land of Zion shall not be accounted as a wise steward this also is an ensample Amen——
