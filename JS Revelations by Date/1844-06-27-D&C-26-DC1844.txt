[heading] Section 49 • Revelation, July 1830–B D&C 26 SECTION XLIX. Revelation to Joseph Smith, jr. Oliver Cowdery and John Whitmer, given July, 1830.

1
Behold, I say unto you, that you shall let your time be devoted to the studying of the S criptures, and to preaching, and to confirming the church at Colesville; and to performing your labors on the land, such as is required, until after you shall go to the west, to hold the next conference; and then it shall be made known what you shall do.
2
And all things shall be done by common consent in the church, by much prayer and faith; for all things you shall receive by faith: Amen.
