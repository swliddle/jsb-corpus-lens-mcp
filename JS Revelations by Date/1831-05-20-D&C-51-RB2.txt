1 Revelation, 20 May 1831 [D&C 51] Thomp[s]on August <May>, 20, 1831
2 Hearken unto me saith the <L>ord your God <and> I will speak unto my <s>erv<a>nt Edward [Partridge] <and> give unto him directions for it must needs be that he receive directions how to organise this people
3 for it must needs be that they are organized according to my laws if otherwise they will be cut off
4 wherefore let my <s>ervant Edward receive the properties of this <p>eople which have covenanted with me to obey the Laws which I have given <and> let my <s>erv<a>nt Edward receive the money as it shall be laid before him according to the covenant <and> go <and> obtain a deed or <a>rticle of this land unto himself <of him who holdeth it if he harden not his heart > for I have appointed him to receive these things <and> thus through him the <p>roperties of th<e> Church shall be <consecrated> unto me

6 <and> let my serv<a>nt Edward when he shall appoint a man his portion give unto him a writing that shall secure unto him his portion that he shall hold it of the <c>hurch until he transgress <and> is not counted worthy by the voice of the <c>hurch according to the <L>aws to belong to the <c>hurch

8 <and> thus all things shall be made sure according to the <L>aws of the land
9 <and> let that which belongeth to this people be appointed unto this people

11 <and> let every man deal honestly <and> be alike among this <p>eople <and> receive alike that ye may be one even as I have commanded you
12 <and> let that which belongeth to this people not be taken <and> given unto that of another church

18 <and> I consecrate unto them this land for a little season until I the Lord shall provide for them otherwise <and> command them to go hence
19 <and> the hour and the day is not given unto them wherefore let them act upon this land as for years <and> this shall turn unto them for their good
20 <b>ehold this shall be an example unto my <s>erv<a>nt Edward in other places in all <c>hurches
