1 Revelation, 25 December 1832 [D&C 87] Kirtlnd, July 3, 1835.
2 Verily thus saith the Lord, concerning the wars that will shortly come to pass, beginning <and arising out of > rebellion<s like unto the one> of South Carolina: <and it >will eventually <come to pass, that war shall brake out and> te[r]minate in the death and miseary of many souls;
3 and the days will come that war will be poured out upon all nations<, and it shall go forth, >beginning upon this continent;
4 <for there will be a division among the people; > for behold the southern states shall be divided against the northern states, <and other nations will be stirred up unto war also, > even the nation of Great Britain, as it is called; and they shall also call upon other nations, in order to defend themselves aga[i]nst other nations; and <thus> war shall be pourd out upon all nations.
5 And it shall come to pass after m[a]ny days, slaves <those who are held in bondage >shall rise up <against those who hold them in bondage, and > shall be martialed and disiplined for war.
6 And it shall come to pass also, that the remnants who are left of the land, will martial themselves <and also > shall become exceeding angery, and shall vex the Gentiles with a sore vexing vexation.
7 And then with the sword, and by bloodshed, the inhabitants of the earth shall mourn; and with famine and plague, and earthquakes, and the thunder of heaven, and the fierce and vivid lightning also, shall the inhabinants of the earth be made to feell the wrath and indignation <and> of the chastning hand of an Almighty God, until the consumption decreed hath made a full end of all nations,
8 that the cry of the saints, and of the blood of the saints, shall cease to come up into the ears of the Lord of Sabaoth, from the earth, to be avenged of their enemies.
9 Wherefore, stand ye in holy places and be not moved until the day of the Lord come; for behold, it cometh quickly, saith the Lord. Amen.
