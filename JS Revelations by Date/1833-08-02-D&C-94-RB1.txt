[heading] Revelation, 2 August 1833–B D&C 94

1
And again verily I say unto you my friends a commandment I give unto ye that ye shall commence a work of laying out & preparing a begining & foundation of the & City of the Stake of Zion here in the land of Kirtland begining at my house
2
& behold it must be done according to the pattern which I have given unto you
3
& let the first lot on the south be consecrated unto me for the building of an house for the Presidency in obtaining revelations & for the work of the ministery of the Presedency in all things partaining to the Church & Kingdom
4
verily I say unto you that it shall be built fifty five by sixty five in the width thereof & in the length thereof in the inner court
5
& there shall be a lower Court & a higher court according to the pattern which shall be given unto you hereafter
6
& it shall be dedicated unto the Lord from the foundation thereof according to the order of the priesthood according to the pattern which shall be given unto you hereafter
7
& it shall be wholly dedicated unto the Lord for the word work of the Presidency
8
& ye shall not suffer any unclean thing to come in into it & my glory shall be there & my presence shall be there
9
but if there shall come into it any unclean thing my glory shall not be there & my presence shall not come in to it.
10
And again verily I say unto you the second lot on the South shall be dedicated unto me for the work of the printing of my the translation of my Scriptures & all things whatsoever I shall command you
11
& it shall be fifty five by Sixty five in the width thereof & in the length thereof in the inner court & there & shall be a lower & a higher court
12
& this house shall be wholly dedicated unto the Lord from the foundation thereof for the work of the Printing in all things whatsoever I shall command you to be holy & undefiled in according to the pattern in all things as it shall be given unto you
13
& on the third lot shall my Servant Hyrum (Smith) receive his inheritance
14
& on the first & second lots on the north shall my Servant Reynolds Cahoon & Jarad Jared Carter receive their inheritance
15
that they may do the work which I have appointed unto them, to be a committee to build my house according to the commandment which I the Lord God have given unto you
16
[not present in this edition]
17
& now I give unto you no more at this time.
