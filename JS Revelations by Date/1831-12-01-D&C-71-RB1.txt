[heading] Revelation, 1 December 1831 D&C 71 82 A Revelation to Joseph & Sidney Rigdon given Hiram Portage County Ohio Decm. 1, 1831

1
Behold thus saith the Lord unto you my servants that the time has verily come that it is nessessary & expedient in me that that you should open your mouths in proclaming my Gospel the things of the Kingdom expounding the Misteries thereof out of the Scriptures according to that portion of spirit & power which shall be given unto you even as I will
2
verily I say unto you proclaim unto the world in the regions round about & in the Church also for the space of a season even untill it shall be made known unto you
3
verily this is a mission for a season which I give unto you
4
wherefore labor in my Church vineyard call upon the inhabitants of the Earth & bear record & prepare the way for the Commandments & the Revelations which are to come
5
Now behold this is wisdom whoso readeth let him understand & receive also
6
for unto him who receiveth it shall be given more abundantly even Power,
7
Wherefore confound your enemies call upon them to meet you both in publick & in private & in as much as ye are faithful their shame shall be made manifest
8
Wherefore let them bring forth their strong reasons against the Lord
9
Verily thus saith the Lord unto you there is no weapon that is formed against you that shall prosper
10
& if any man lift his voice against you he shall be confounded in mine own due time
11
Wherefore keep these commandments for they are true & faithful even so amen
