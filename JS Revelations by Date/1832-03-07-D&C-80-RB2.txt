[heading] Revelation, 7 March 1832 D&C 80 Hiram Portage County Ohio March 7th 1832

1
Verily thus saith the Lord unto you my sevent servant Stephen Burnett go ye go ye into the world & preach the Gospel to every creature that cometh under the Sound of your voice
2
and inasmuch as you desire a companion I will give unto you my servant Eden Smith
3
therefore go ye and preach my gospel whither to the North or to the South, to the East or to the west it mattereth not for you cannot go amiss
4
therefore declare the things which you have heard and verily believe and know to be true.
5
Behold this is the will of him who hath called you Your Redeemer even Jesus Christ Amen
