1 Revelation, 1 August 1831 [D&C 58] 61 Commandment August 1st. 1831 A Revelation given to the Elders who were assembeled in on the land of Zion Directions what to do &c &c &c
2 Hearken oh ye Elders of my Church & give ear to my word & learn of me what I will concerning you & also concerning this land unto which I have sent you
3 for verily I say unto you blessed is he that keepeth my commandments whether in life or in death & he that is faithfull in tribulation the reward of the same is greater in the kingdom of heaven
4 ye cannot behold with your natural eyes for the present time the design of your God concerning those things which shall come hereafter & the glory which shall follow after much tribulation
5 for after much tribulation cometh the blessings Wherefore, the day cometh that ye shall be crowned with much glory the hour is not yet but is nigh at hand
6 remember this which I tell you before that you may lay it to heart & receive that which shall follow
7 behold verily I say unto you for this cause I have sent you that you might be obedient & that your hearts might be prepared to bear testimony of the things which are to come
8 & also that you might be honoured of laying the foundation & of bearing record of the land upon which the Zion of God shall stand
9 & also that a feast of fat things might be prepared for the poor yea a feast of fat things of wine on the lees well refined that the earth may know that the mouths of the Prophets shall not fail
10 yea a supper of the house of the Lord well prepared unto which all nations shall be invited
11 firstly the rich & the learned the wise & the Noble
12 & after that cometh the day of my Power then shall the poor the lame and the blind & the deaf come in unto the marriage of the lamb & partake of the supper of the Lord prepared for the great day to come
13 Behold I the Lord have spoken it
14 & that the testimony might go forth from Zion yea from the mouth of the City of the heritage of God
15 yea for this cause I have Sent you hither & have Selected my Servent Edward [Partridge] & appointed him his mission in this land
16 but if he repent not of his sins which is unbelief & blindness of heart let him take heed lest he fall
17 behold his mission is given unto him & it shall not be given again
18 & whoso standeth in that mission is appointed to be a Judge in Israel like as it was in ancient days to divide the lands of the heritage of God unto his children
19 & to Judge his people by the testimony of the Just & by the assistance of his councillors according to the laws of the kingdom which are given by the Prophets of God
20 for verily I say unto you my laws shall be kept on this land
21 let no man think that he is ruler but let god rule him that Judgeth according to the council of his own will (or in other words) him that councileth or seteth upon the Judgement Seat
22 let no man break the laws of the land for he that keepeth the laws of God hath no need to break the laws of the land
23 Wherefore be subject to the powers that be untill he reigns whose right it is to reign & subdues all enemies under his feet
24 behold the laws which ye have received from my hand are the laws of the Church & in this light ye shall hold them forth behold here is wisdom
25 & now as I spoke concerning my Servent Edward this land is the land of his residence & those whom he has appointed for his councillors & also the land of the residence of him whom I have appointed to keep my storehouse
26 Wherefore let them bring their families to this land as they shall council between them & me
27 for behold it is not meet that I should command in all things for he that is compelled in all things the same is a slothfull & not a wise Servent Wherefore he receiveth no reward
28 verily I say men should be anxiously engaged in a good cause and do many things of their own free will, & bring to pass much righteousness
29 for the power is in them wherein they are agents unto themselves & in-as-much as men are good they shall in no wise loose their reward
30 but he that doeth not any thing untill he is commanded & receiveth a commandment with a doubtfull heart & keepeth it with slothfullness the same is damned
31 Who am I that made man saith the Lord that will hold him guilty that obey not my commandments
32 who am I saith the Lord that have promised & have not fulfilled
33 I command & a man obeys not I revoke & they receive not the blessing
34 then they say in their hearts this is not the work of the Lord for his promises are not fulfilled but wo unto such for their reward lurketh beneath & not from above
35 & now I give unto you further directions concerning this Land
36 it is wisdom in me that my servent Martin [Harris] should be an example unto the church in laying his money before the bishop of the Church
37 & also this is a law unto every man that cometh unto the Land to receive an inheritance and he shall do with his moneys according as the law directs
38 & it is wisdom also that it there should be lands purchased in Independence for the place of the storehouse & also for the house of the Printing
39 & other directions concerning my servent Martin shall be given him of the spirit that he may receive his inheritance as seemeth him good
40 & let him repent of his sins for he seeketh the praise of the world
41 & also let my servent William [W. Phelps] stand in the office which I have appointed him & receive his inheritance in the Land
42 & also he hath need to repent for I the lord am not pleased with him for he seeketh to excell & he is not sufficiently humble meek in his heart
43 behold he that hath repented of his sins the same is forgiven & I the Lord remembereth them no more
44 by this ye may know if a man repenteth of his sins behold he will confess them & forsake them
45 & now verily I say concerning the residue of the Elders of my Church the time has not yet come for many years for them to receive their inheritance in this land except they desire it through prayer only as they it shall be appointed unto them
46 for Behold they shall push the people together from the ends of the Earth
47 wherefore assemble yourselves together, & he that is not appointed to stay in this land let them preach the gospel in the regions round about & after that let them return to their homes
48 let them preach by the way & bear testimony of the truth in all places & call upon the rich the high & the low & the poor to repent
49 & let them build up churches in-as-much as the inhabitants of the Earth will repent
50 & let there be an agent appointed by the voice of the Church
51 & I give unto my servent Sidney [Rigdon] a commandment that he shall write a discription of the Land of Zion & a statement of the will of God as it shall be made known by the spirit unto him
52 and an Epistle & subscription to be presented unto all the Churches to obtain money to be put into the hands of the Bishop to purchase lands for an inheritance for the children of God of himself or the agent as seemeth him good or as he shall direc
53 for behold verily I say unto you the Lord willeth that the deciples & the children of men should open their hearts even to purchase this whole region of country as soon as time will permit
54 behold here is wisdom let them do this lest they reserve none inheritance save it be by the sheding of blood
55 & again in as much as there is lands obtained let there be workmen sent forth of all kinds unto this land to labour for the saints of God
56 let all these things be done in order & let the priveliges of the lands be made known from time to time by the Bishop or the agent of the Church
57 & let the work of the gethering be not in haste nor by flight but let it be done as it shall be councelied by the Elders of the Church at the conferences according to the knoweledge which they receive from time to time
58 & let my servent Sidney consecrate & dedicate this land & the spot of the temple unto the Lord
59 & let a conference meeting be called & after that let my servent Sidney & Joseph return & also Oliver [Cowdery] with them to accomplish the residue of the work which I have appointed unto them in their own land & the residue as shall be ruled by the conference
60 & let no man return from this land except he bear record by the way of that which he knows & most assuredly believes
61 let that which has been bestowed upon Ziba [Peterson] be taken from him & let him stand as a member in the Church & labour with his own hands with the brethren untill he is sufficiently chastened for all his sins for he confeseth them not & he thinketh to hide them
62 let the residue of the Elders of this church which are coming to this land some of whom are exceedingly blessed even above measure also hold a conference
63 which shall be held by them
64 & let them also return preaching the gospel by the way bearing record of the things which are revealed unto them
65 for verily the sound must go forth from this place into all the world & unto the uttermost parts of the Earth the gospel must be preached unto every creature with signs following them that believe
66 & behold the son of man cometh Amen
