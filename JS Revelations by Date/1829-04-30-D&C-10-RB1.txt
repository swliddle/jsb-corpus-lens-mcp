44 Shall publish it as the Record of Nephi & thus I will confound those which have altered my words
45 I will not suffer that they shall destroy my work yea I will sh<o>w unto them that my wisdom is greater than the cuning of the Devil
46 Behold thy they have only got a part or an abridgement of the account of Nephi
47 Behold there are many things engraven on the Plates of Nephi which do throw greater views upon my Gospel therefore it is wisdom in me that ye should translate this first part of the engravings of Nephi & send forth in this work
48 & behold all the remainder of this work doth contain all those parts of my Gospel which the <my> Holy Prophets yea & also my Deciples desired in their Prayers should come forth unto this People &
49 I said unto them that it should be granted unto them according to their faith in their Prayers
50 yea & this is their faith that my Gospel which I give unto them that they might Preach in their days might come unto their Brethren the Lamanites & also all that had become Lamanites because of their dissentions
51 now this is not all <but> their faith in their Prayers where [were] that this Gospel should be made known also if it were posible that other Nations should p[o]ssess the Land
52 & thus they did leave a blessing upon this Land in their prayers that whosoever should believe in this Gospel in this land might have Eternal life
53 yea that it might be free unto all of whatsoever Nation Kindred Tongue or People they may be
54 & now Behold according to their faith in their Prayers will I bring this part of my Gospel to the knowledge of my People Behold I do not bring it to destroy that which they have received but to build it up
55 & for this cause have I said if this generation harden not their hearts I will establish my Church among them
56 now I do not say this to destroy my Church but I say this to build up my Church
57 therefore whosoever belongeth to my Church need not fear for such shall inherit the Kingdom of Heaven
58 but it is they which do not fear me neither keep my commandments but buildeth up Churches unto themselves to get gain yea & all those that do wickedly & buildeth up the Kingdom of the Devil yea Verily Verily I say unto you it is they that I will disturb disturb & cause to tremble & shake to the center
59 Behold I am Jesus Christ the Son of God I came unto my own & my own received Me not
60 I am the light which shineth in darkness & the darkness comprehendeth it not
61 I am he which said other sheep have I which are not of this fold unto my Deciples & many there were which understood me not
62 & I will shew unto this <People> that People that I had other sheep & that they were a branch of the House of Jacob
63 & I will bring to light these marvelous works which they did in my name
64 yea & I will also bring to light my Gospel which was ministered unto them & behold they shall not deny that which ye have received but they shall build it up & shall bring to light the true points of my doctrine yea & the only doctrine which is in me
65 & this I do that I establish my Gospel that there may not be so much contention yea Satan doth Stir up the hearts of the People to contention concerning the points of my doctrine & in these things they do err for they do arrest the Scriptures & do not understand them
66 therefore I will unfold unto them this great mystery
67 for Behold I will gether them to ge as a hen gethereth her Chickens under her wings if they will not harden their hearts
68 yea if they will come they may & partake of the waters of life freely
69 Behold this is my doctrine whosoever repenteth & cometh unto me the same is my Church
70 whos[o]ever declareth more or less then this is not of me but is against me therefore he is not of my Church
71 now Behold whosoever is of my Church & & endureth my church to the end him will I establish upon my rock & the gates of Hell shall not prevail against him them
72 & now remember the words of who has the life & the light of the world your redeemer your Lord & your God Amen—
