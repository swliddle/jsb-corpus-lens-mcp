1 Revelation, 30 August 1831 [D&C 63] 66 Commandment received at Kirtland Geauga Ohio August 30th 1831 to the Church &c
2 Hearken, oh ye People & open your hearts & give ear from a-far & listen you that call yourselves the people of God the Lord & hear the word of the Lord & his will concerning you
3 yea verily I say hear the word of him whose anger is kindled against the wicked & rebelious
4 who willeth to take even whom he will take & preserveth in life them whom he will preserve
5 who buildeth up at his own will & pleasure & destroys when he please & is able to cast the soul down to hell
6 Behold I the Lord uttereth my voice & it shall be obeyed
7 wherefore verily I say let the wicked take heed & let the rebelious fear & tremble & let the unbelieving hold their lips for the day of wrath shall come upon them as a whirlwind & all flesh shall know that I am god
8 & he that seeketh signs shall see signs but not unto salvation
9 verily I say unto you there are those among you who who seeketh signs & there has been such even from the begining
10 but behold faith cometh not by signs but signs follow those that believe
11 yea signs cometh by faith not by the will of men nor as they please but by the will of God
12 yea signs cometh by faith unto mighty works for without faith no man pleaseth God & with whom god is angery he is not well pleased wherefore unto such he sheweth no signs only in wrath in condemnation
13 wherefore I the Lord am not pleased with those among you who have sought after signs & wonders & not for faith & not for the good of men unto my glory
14 nevertheless I gave commandments & many have turned away from my commandments & have not kept them
15 there were among you adulters & adultereses some of whom have turned away from you & others remain with you that hereafter shall be revealed
16 let such be aware & repent speedily lest Judgement shall come upon you them as a snare & their folly shall be made manifest & their works shall follow them in the eyes of the People
17 & verily I say unto you as I have said before he that looketh on a woman to lust after her or if any shall commit adultery in his heart they shall not have the spirit but shall deny the faith & shall fear
18 wherefore I the Lord have said that the fearfull & the unbelieving & all liars & whosoever loveth & maketh a lie & the whoremonger & the sorcerer should have their part in that lake which burneth with fire & brimstone which is the second death
19 verily I say that they shall not have part in the first resurrection
20 & now behold I the lord saieth unto you that ye are not Justified because these things are among you
21 nevertheless he that endureth in faith & doeth my will the same shall overcome & shall receive an inheritance upon the earth when the day of transfiguration shall come
22 when the earth shall be transfigured even according to the pattern shewn Which was shewn unto mine Apostles upon the mount of which account the fullness ye have not yet received
23 & now verily I say unto you that as I said that I would make it known unto you not by the way of commandment for there are many who observe not to keep my commandments
24 but unto him that keepeth my commandments I will give the mysteries of my kingdom & the same shall be in him a well of living water springing up unto eternal <everlasting> life
25 & now behold this is the will of the lord your god concerning his saints that they should assemble themselves together unto the land of Zion not in haste lest there should be confusion which bringeth pestilence
26 behold the land of Zion I the Lord holdeth it in mine own hands
27 nevertheless I the Lord rendereth unto Sezar [Caesar] the things which are Seazers
28 wherefore I the Lord willeth that you should purchase the Lands that you may have advantage of the world that you may have claim on the world that they may not be stirred up unto anger
29 for satan puteth it into their hearts to anger & to the sheding of blood
30 wherefore the land of Zion shall not be obtained but by purchase or by blood otherwise there is none inheritance for you
31 & if by purchase behold you are blessed
32 & if by blood as ye are forbidden to shed blood Lo your enemies are upon you & ye shall be scourged from city to city & from Synegogue to Synegogue & but few shall stand to receive an inheritance
33 I the Lord am angery with the wicked I am holding my spirit from the inhabitants of the Earth
34 I have sworn in my wrath & decreed wars upon the face of the Earth & the wicked shall slay the wicked & fear shall c ome upon every man
35 & the saints also shall hardly escape nevertheless I the Lord am with them & will come down in heaven heaven from the presence of god & consume the wicked with unquenchable fire
36 & behold this is not yet but by & by
37 wherefore seeing that I the Lord have decreed all these things upon the face of the Earth I willeth that my saints should be assembelled upon the Land of Zion
38 & that every man should take righteousness in his hands & faithfullness upon his loins & lift a warning voice unto the inhabitants of the Earth & declare both by word & by flight that desolation shall come upon the wicked
39 wherefore let my Deciples in Kirtland arrange their temporal concerns which Dwell upon this farm
40 let my servent Titas [Titus Billings] who has the care thereof dispose of the Land that he may be prepared in the following <coming> spring to take his Journey up unto the land of Zion with those that dwell upon the face thereof excepting those whom I shall reserve unto myself that shall not go untill I command them
41 & let all the moneys which can be spared (it mattereth not unto me whether it be little or much) sent up unto the Land of Zion unto them whom I have appointed to receive
42 behold I the Lord will give unto my Servent Joseph power that he shall be enabled to decern by the spirit those who shall go up unto the Land of Zion & those of my Deciples which shall tarry
43 let my Servent Newel [K.] Whitney retain his store or in other words his store yet for a little season
44 nevertheless let him impart all the money which he can impart to be sent up unto the Land of Zion
45 behold these things are in his own hands let him <do> according to wisdom
46 verily I say let him be ordained as an agent unto the deciples that shall tarry & let him be ordaned unto this power
47 & now speedily visit the churches expounding these things unto them with my servent Oliver [Cowdery] Behold this is my will obtaining moneys even as I have directed
48 he that is faithfull & endureth shall overcome the world
49 he that sendeth up treasures unto the Land of Zion shall receive an inheritance in this world & his works shall follow him & also a reward in the world to come
50 yea & blessed are the dead that die in the Lord from hence forth when the Lord shall come & old things shall pass away & all things become new they shall rise from the dead & shall not die & shall receive an inheritance before the Lord in the holy City
51 & he that liveth when the Lord shall come & have kept the faith blessed is he nevertheless it is appointed to him to die at the age of man
52 wherefore children shall grow up untill they become old. old men shall die but they shall not sleep in the dust but they shall be changed in the twinkling of an eye
53 wherefore for this cause preached the apostles unto the world the resurrection of the dead
54 these things are the things that ye must look for & speaking after the manner of the Lord they are now nigh at hand & in a time to come even in the day of the coming of the son of man
55 & untill that time there will be foolish virgins among the wise & at that hour cometh an entire seperation of the righteous & the wicked & in that day will I send mine angels to pluck out the wicked & cast them into unquenchable fire
56 & now behold verily I say unto you I the Lord am not pleased with my servent Sidney [Rigdon] he exhalteth himself in his heart & received not in atr◊◊tion Council but grieved the spirit
57 wherefore his writing is not exceptable unto the Lord & he shall make another & if the Lord receive it not he standeth no longer in the office which he hath appointed him
58 & again verily I say unto you let those who desire in their hearts in meekness to warn sinners to repentance let them be ordained unto this power
59 for this is a day of warning & not a day of many words for I the Lord am not to be mocked in the last days
60 behold I am from above & my power lieth beneath I am over all & in all & searcheth all things & the days cometh that all things shall be subject unto me
61 Behold I am alpha & Omega even Jesus christ wherefore let all men be aware how they take my name in their lips
62 for behold verily I say that meny there be who are under this condemnation who useth the name of the Lord & useth it in vain having not authority
63 wherefore let the Church repent & I the Lord will own them otherwise they shall be cut off
64 remember that. That <which> cometh from above is sacred & must be spoken with care & by constraint of the spirit & in this there is no condemnation & ye receive the spirit through prayer wherefore without this there remaineth condemnation
65 let my servent Joseph & Sidney seek them a home as they are taught through prayer by the spirit
66 these things rema[i]n to over come through patience that such may receive a more exceeding & eternal weight of glory otherwise a greater condemnation Amen
