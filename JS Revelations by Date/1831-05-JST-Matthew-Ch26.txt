1And it came to pass, when Jesus had
finished all these sayings he said unto
his disciples, 2“You know that after
two days is the Passover, and the Son is
to be betrayed and crucified.”

elders of the people unto the palace of

4and consulted that they might take

the feast day.”
6Now when Jesus was in Bethany
in the house of Simon the leper, 7there

and poured it on his head as he sat in
the house.
8But when his disciples saw her they

“To what purpose is this waste? 9For

much and given to the poor.”
10And when they had thus reasoned
among themselves and understood
not, Jesus knowing their hearts, he

said unto them, “Why trouble you the

your hearts? For verily I say unto you,
she hath wrought a good work upon
me. 11For the poor you have always
with you, but me you have not always.
12This woman hath poured this oint-
