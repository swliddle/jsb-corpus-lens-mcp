[heading] A commandment unto the church of Christ which was established in these last days A. D. 1830, on the 4th month and the 6th day of the month, which is called April:—

1
Behold I say unto you that all old covenants have I caused to be done away in this thing, and this is a new and everlasting covenant;
2
wherefore, although a man should be baptized a hundred times it availeth him nothing, for ye cannot enter in at the straight gait by the law of Moses, neither by your dead works,
3
for it is because of your dead works that I have caused this last covenant and this church to be built up unto me;
4
wherefore enter ye in at the gate as I have commanded, and seek not to counsel your God.
