NOTES: Secondhand account of the First Vision recorded in the journal of Alexander Neibaur on 24 May 1844, just weeks before JS's martyrdom. Neibaur was JS's dentist in Nauvoo and a close associate. This journal entry is the latest known account of the First Vision recorded during JS's lifetime. This is the EVENT-DATE copy (1820-04). The WRITTEN-DATE copy is filed under 1844-05-24. This account is notable for several unique details not found in other accounts, including the specific mention of the revival preacher's text ("Come out of her my people"), the description of a "fire" in the grove, and the detail that the first personage (God the Father) said "This is my beloved Son, hear him" while pointing to the other personage.

═══ HISTORICAL INTRODUCTION ═══

Alexander Neibaur was a native of Alsace, France, who converted to the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in England in 1838. He was a dentist and an educated man who spoke several languages. After emigrating to Nauvoo, Illinois, he became JS's personal dentist and a close friend. Neibaur kept a journal in which he recorded this account of the First Vision after JS personally recounted it to him on 24 May 1844, less than five weeks before JS's assassination on 27 June 1844. This entry is the last known contemporary record of JS narrating his first vision. Neibaur's account includes details not found in any other version, suggesting that JS continued to share new details and emphases even in the last weeks of his life.

═══ ACCOUNT TEXT ═══

[Friday 24 May 1844]
Bro Joseph told us the first call he had a Revival Meeting, his Mother & Bro & Sist got Religion. he wanted to get Religion too— wanted to feel & shout like the Rest but could feel nothing. opened his Bible & the first Passage that Struck him was "if any man lack Wisdom let him ask of God who giveth to all Men liberality & upbraideth not"— went into the Wood to pray— kneeled himself down his tongue was closet— cleavet to his roof— could not utter a single word, felt easier after a while— tried again could not make any sound but heard a sound like some one walking towards him— sprung up on his feet & opened his eyes but could not see any person. kneeled again— tried to pray— the noise of walking seemed to draw nearer — he sprung up and nothing but darkness surrounded him on every side— then a light from above more brighter than the sun, descended gracefully and gradually until it fell upon the tops of the trees, and more and more glory was spread within it— he expected every moment the trees would be consumed but not even a leaf was injured— it was of a very great glory for the leaves of the trees did not so much as move; it continued descending gradually until it rested upon him— an Angel appeared who said unto him that his sins were forgiven him— he said unto him also, that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.— & he saw many Angels in this vision. I shall not go into this at this Time but record the substance of what was communicated to him by the Angel, it was of the greatest importance.—

Bro Joseph was here referred to a former occasion when he prayed and received a promise that the fulness of the truth should be revealed to him at some future time. A personage appeared in the midst of this pillar of Flame, which was spread all around and yet nothing consumed— Another personage soon appeared like unto the first— he said unto him Joseph my son thy Sins are forgiven thee, go thy way walk in my Statutes and keep my Commandments— behold I am the Lord of Glory I was Crucified for the world that all those who believe on my Name may have Eternal life— behold the world lieth in Sin at this time and none doeth good no not one— they have turned aside from the gospel and keep not my Commandments— they draw near to me with their Lips while their hearts are far from me— mine anger is kindling against the inhabitants of the Earth to visit them according to their ungodliness and to bring to pass that which hath been spoken by the mouth of the Prophets and Apostles— behold and lo I come quickly as it is written of me in the Cloud clothed in the glory of my Father—
