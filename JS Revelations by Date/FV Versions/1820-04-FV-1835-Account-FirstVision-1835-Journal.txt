NOTES: Joseph Smith's second known firsthand account of the First Vision, recorded by scribe Warren Parrish in JS's journal on 9 November 1835 during a visit from Robert Matthews ("Joshua, the Jewish minister"). This is the EVENT-DATE copy (1820-04). The WRITTEN-DATE copy is filed under 1835-11-09. This account uniquely mentions seeing "many angels" and describes a "pillar of fire" rather than "pillar of light." It emphasizes the witness that "Jesus Christ is the son of God."

═══ HISTORICAL INTRODUCTION ═══

The journal records Joseph Smith's activities during a period when he viewed himself as divinely commissioned to gather God's people and prepare for the Second Coming, with the Kirtland Temple as the centerpiece. The November 9, 1835 entry describes a visit to JS by Robert Matthews, more commonly known as the Prophet Matthias. His visit prompted JS to relate, as JS characterized it, a "brief history of the establishment of the Church of Christ in these last days." A visitor later in the week heard a similar recounting, though one recorded in much less detail.
