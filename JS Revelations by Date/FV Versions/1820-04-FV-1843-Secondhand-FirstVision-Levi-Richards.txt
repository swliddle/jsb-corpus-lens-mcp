═══ HISTORICAL INTRODUCTION ═══

Levi Richards was a physician and member of the Church who kept a detailed journal. On 11 June 1843, he recorded a discourse given by JS in Nauvoo, Illinois. In this discourse, JS recounted portions of his early history, including his first vision. Richards's journal entry provides a brief but important contemporaneous record of JS publicly sharing his First Vision experience in the early 1840s.

═══ ACCOUNT TEXT ═══

[Sunday 11 June 1843]
Attended meeting. Prest J. preached— There is a God in Heaven who is infinite & eternal.— from before the Creation of the world he ordained the plan for our Salvation— before we came here. . . . he was once as one of us & was on a planet as Jesus was in the flesh— so he had to have a plan & bring it about.— & he does it by making known the plan of salvat. to Son & to us.— He first went into a grove to pray & saw a light & was ministered unto— he opened the heavens upon him & he said he saw the Lord— Said that God had set the ordinances to be the same forever & ever & set Adam to watch over them to reveal them from Heaven to man—or to send Angels to reveal them.
