Next Meetting on Mechanical Powers & utility— on Virtue— on the Results of Eivel—
Joseph said Concerning [p. [11]]

the God-head it was Not as many imagined— three Heads & but one body; he said the three were separate bodys, God the first & Jesus the Mediator the 2d & the Holy Ghost & these three agree in one & this is the manner we should aproach God in order to get his blessings & he also said Every man is stimulated by a certain motive, motive preceeds action & if we want to know ourselves this is the key to Examine the motive what it is, & the fact will be manifest [p. [12]]
