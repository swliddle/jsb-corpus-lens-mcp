1 Section 23 • Revelation, 20 May 1831 [D&C 51] SECTION XXIII. Revelation given May, 1831.
2 <1 >Hearken unto me<,> saith the Lord your God<,> and I will speak unto my servant Edward Partridge<,> and give unto him directions<:> for it must needs be that he receive directions how to organize this people<:>
3 for it must needs be that they are organized according to my laws<,> if otherwise they will be cut off<:>
4 [not present in this edition]
5 wherefore let my servant Edward <Partridge, >and those whom he has chosen<,> in whom I am well pleased<,> appoint unto this people their portion<,> every man <equal> according to their families<,> according to <their circumstances,> and their wants <and> needs<;>
6 and let my servant Edward <Partridge,> when he shall appoint a man his portion<,> give unto him a writing that shall secure unto him his portion<,> that he shall hold it<, even this right and this inheritance in> the church<,> until he transgress<es> and is not <ac>counted worthy by the voice of the church<,> according to the <l>aws <and covenants of the church,> to belong to the church<:>
7 <and if he shall transgress, and is not accounted worthy to belong to the church, he shall not have power to claim that portion which he has consecrated unto the bishop for the poor and the needy of my church: therefore, he shall not retain the gift, but shall only have claim on that portion that is deeded unto him.>
8 <A>nd thus<,> all things shall be made sure according to the laws of the land<.>
9 <2 A>nd let that which belong<s> to this people<,> be appointed unto this people<;>
10 and the money which is left unto this people<,> let there be an agent appointed unto this people, to take the money to provide food and raiment, according to the wants of this people.
11 <A>nd let every man deal honestly<,> and be alike among this people<,> and receive alike<,> that ye may be one<,> even as I have commanded you<.>
12 <3 A>nd let that which belongeth to this people not be taken and given unto that of another church<;>
13 wherefore if another church would receive mon<e>y of this church<,> let them pay unto this church again according as they shall agree<;>
14 and this shall be done through the <b>ishop or the agent<,> which shall be appointed by the voice of the church<.>
15 <4 A>nd again<,> let the <b>ishop appoint a storehouse unto this church<,> and let all things<,> both in money and in meat<,> which is more than is needful for the want of this people<,> be kept in the hands of the <b>ishop<.>
16 <A>nd let him also reserve unto himself<,> for his own wants<,> and for the wants of his family<,> as he shall be employed in doing this business<.>
17 <A>nd thus I grant unto this people a privil<e>ge of organizing themselves according to my laws<;>
18 and I consecrate unto them this land for a little season<,> until I the Lord shall provide for them otherwise<,> and command them to go hence<;>
19 and the hour and the day is not given unto them<:> wherefore let them act upon this land as for years<;> and this shall turn unto them for their good<.>
20 <5 B>ehold<,> this shall be an example unto my servant Edward <Partridge,> in other places<,> in all churches<.>
21 <A>nd whoso is found a faithful<,> a just and a wise steward<,> shall enter into the joy of his <L>ord<,> and shall inherit eternal life<.>
22 Verily I say unto you<,> I am Jesus Christ<,> who cometh quickly<,> in an hour you think not<: even so. >Amen<.>
