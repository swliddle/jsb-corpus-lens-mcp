═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

Sunday 16 Heard pres. J preach on the resurrection. shewing the importance of being buried with the saints & their relatives inasmuch as we shall [p. 78]

want to see our relatives first & shall rejoice to strike hands with our parents, children &c when rising from the tomb, [p. 79]
