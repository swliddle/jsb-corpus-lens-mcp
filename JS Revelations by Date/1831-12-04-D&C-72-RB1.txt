[heading] Revelation, 4 December 1831–A D&C 72:1–8 81 A Revelation Recd. in Kirtland December 4, 1831 the Aappointment of Bishop his duty &c

1
Hearken & listen to the voice of the Lord O ye who have called yourselves together who are the High Priests of the my Church to whom the Kingdom & Power have been given
2
for verily thus saith the Lord it is expedient in me for a Bishop to be appointed unto you or of you unto the Church in this part of the Lords vinyard
3
& verily in this thing ye have done wisely for it is required of the Lord at the hand of every Stewart to render an account of his Stewartship both in time & in eternity
4
for he who is faithful & wise in time is accounted worthy to inherit the mansions prepared for them of my father
5
verily I say unto you the Elders of the Church in this part of my Vineyard shall render an account of their stewartship unto the Bishop which shall be appointed unto me of me in this part of my vineyard
6
these things shall be had on record to be handed over unto the Bishop in Zion
7
& the duty of the Bishop shall be be made known by the commandments which have been given & by the voice of the conferences
8
& now I say unto you my Servant Newel is the man (K. Whitney) is the man who shall be appointed & ordained unto this power this is the will of the Lord your God your redeemer even so Amen—
9
The duty of the Bishop as made known at the same time The word of the Lord in adition to the law which has been given making known the duty of the Bishops which have has been ordained unto the Church in this Part of the vinyard which is verily this
10
to keep the Lords store house to receive the funds of the Church in this part of the vineyard
11
to take an account of the Elders as before has been commanded & to administer to their wants who shall pay for that which they receive in as much as they have wherewith to pay
12
that this also may be consecrated to the good of the Church to the poor & needy
13
& he who has not wherewith to pay an account shall be taken & handed over to the Bishop in Zion who shall pay the debt out of that which the Lord shall put into his hands
14
& the labours of the faithful who labor in spiritual things in administering the Gospel & the things of the Kingdom unto the Church & unto the World shall answer the debt unto the Bishop in Zion
15
thus it cometh out of the Church for according to the Law every man who cometh up to Zion must lay all things before the Bishop in Zion
16
& now verily I say unto you that as every Elder in this part of the vineyard must give an account of his stewartship unto the Bishop in this part of the vineyard
17
a certificate from the Judge or Bishop in this part of the vineyard unto the Bishop in Zion rendereth every man acceptable & answereth all for an inheritance & to be received as a wise stewart & as a faithful laborer
18
otherwise shall not be acceptable of the Bishop in Zion
19
And now verily I say unto you let every Elder who shall give an account unto the Bishop of the Church in this part of the vineyard be recomended by the Church or Churches in which he labours that he may render himself & his accounts approved in all things
20
And again let my servants who are abroad appointed as stewarts over the literary concerns of my Church have claims for assistance upon the Bishop or Bishops in all things
21
that the Revelations may be published & go forth unto the ends of the Earth that they also may obtain funds which shall benefit the Church in all things
22
that they also may render themselves approved in all things & be accepted as wise stewarts
23
And Behold this shall be an ensample for all the extensive branches of my Church in whatsoever land they shall be established & now I make an end of my sayings amen.
24
A fiew words in adition to the laws of the Kingdom respecting the members of the Church they who are appointed by the Holy spirit to go up unto Zion & they who are privileged to go up unto Zion
25
let them carry up unto the Bishop a certificate from three Elders of the Church or a certificate from the Bishop
26
otherwise he who shall go up unto the land of Zion shall not be accounted a wise stewart this also an ensample amen—
