28[th] Sunday I spent the day at in company with Joseph & the in conversing upon a variety of subjects it was an interesting day was present he had been in four years we also saw a number of english Brethren Joseph said the Book of Mormon was the most correct of any Book on earth & the keystone of our religion & a man would get nearer to God by abiding by its precepts than any other Book [p. [112]]
