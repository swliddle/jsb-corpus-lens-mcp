[heading] Section 60 • Revelation, 6 January 1831 D&C 40 SECTION LX. Revelation to Joseph Smith, jr. and Sidney Rigdon, given January, 1831, explaining why James Covill Covel, obeyed not the revelation which was given unto him.

1
Behold, verily I say unto you, that the heart of my servant James Covill was right before me, for he covenanted with me, that he would obey my word.
2
And he received the word with gladness, but straightway satan tempted him; and the fear of persecution, and the cares of the world, caused him to reject the word;
3
w herefore he broke my covenant, and it remaineth in me to do with him as seemeth me good. Amen.
