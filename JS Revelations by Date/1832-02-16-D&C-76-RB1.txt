[heading] Vision, 16 February 1832 D&C 76 A Vision of Joseph and Sidney Rigdon Febuary the 16. 1832 given in Portage County Hiram Township State of Ohio in North America which they saw concerning the Church of the first born & concerning the coming the coming the of God & his vast creation throughout all eternity.
