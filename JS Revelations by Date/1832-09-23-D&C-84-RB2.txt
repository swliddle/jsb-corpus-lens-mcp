[heading] Revelation, 22–23 September 1832 D&C 84 A Revelation given the 22 & 23d. of Sept. 1832

1
A Revelation of Jesus Christ unto his servant Joseph and six Elders as they united heart there hearts and lifted there voice on high
2

now this Moses plainly taught to the children of Israel in the wilderness and saught dilligently to sanctify his people that they might behold the face of God,

for he was baptized while he was yet in the womb, and was ordained by the Angel of God at the time he was eight days old unto this power, to overthrow the kingdom of the Jews and to make straight the way of the Lord before the face of his people to prepare them for the coming of the Lord, in whose hand is given all power;
