came down from heaven—the Son of
Man, who is in heaven.

to condemn the world but that the
world through him might be saved.

demned, but he who believeth not is
condemned already because he hath
not believed on the name of the Only
Begotten Son of God, which before
was preached by the mouth of the holy
prophets, for they testified of me.

that light is come into the world, and
men love darkness rather than light
because their deeds are evil. 20For
everyone who doeth evil hateth the
light, neither cometh to the light lest
his deeds should be reproved. 21But he
who loveth truth cometh to the light,
that his deeds may be made manifest.
And he who obeyeth the truth, the
works which he doeth, they are of
God.”
