═══ DISCOURSE TEXT ═══

Tues 13th Last sunday I had the privilege of attending meeting and in the forenoon listening to a very interesting discourse by J. Smith He took for his subject the words of the Savior to wit. “O Jerusalem thou that killest the prophets and stonest them that are sent unto you! How oft would I have gathered you as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings and you would not!” He beautifully and in a most powerful manner, illustrated the necessity of the and the building of the that those may be administered which are [p. [34]]

necessary preparations for the world to come: he exhorted the people in impressive terms to be diligent— to be up and doing lest the tabernacle pass over to another people and we lose the blessings. [p. [35]]
